# NOVALYTE WORKFORCE — ECOSYSTEM-INTEGRATED HEALTHCARE WORKFORCE PLATFORM

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Redesign and expand the existing Novalyte Workforce experience so it becomes the workforce, hiring, talent-discovery, and flexible-staffing layer within the broader Novalyte healthcare ecosystem.

Do not position Workforce as an unrelated standalone product.

Do not limit Workforce exclusively to men’s health.

Novalyte Workforce must remain structurally, visually, and strategically connected to:

- Patient acquisition
- Clinic growth
- Clinic directory
- Healthcare marketplace
- Clinic and organization profiles
- Clinic and employer dashboards
- Healthcare professional profiles
- Job publishing
- Hiring workflows
- Applicant tracking
- Flexible staffing
- Workforce-capacity expansion

The platform should support the broader healthcare market while maintaining a clear connection to Novalyte’s initial focus on men’s health clinics.

Do not create arbitrary one-off colors, typography, spacing, buttons, cards, form styles, or interface patterns.

Do not replace the existing approved brand palette with visually similar custom colors.

Where existing shared components are appropriate, reuse them.

Where new components are necessary, build them using the same tokens, visual language, interaction behavior, and accessibility standards used throughout the existing application.

The Workforce page must feel like a native part of Novalyte—not a separate template, microsite, or third-party job board.

# CORE ECOSYSTEM STRATEGY

Novalyte operates as one connected healthcare ecosystem.

The relationship between the platform’s major components should be clearly communicated:

1. Patient acquisition creates qualified patient demand for healthcare organizations.
2. Clinics and providers receive more patient opportunities.
3. Growing organizations require additional clinical and operational capacity.
4. Novalyte Workforce helps organizations find permanent, part-time, contract, temporary, and flexible talent.
5. Novalyte Marketplace helps organizations obtain equipment, technology, services, supplies, and operational resources.
6. The Clinic Directory improves provider visibility and patient discovery.
7. Organization dashboards connect patient-growth workflows, job listings, applicants, marketplace activity, and future operational capabilities.

The Workforce page should communicate that it is part of this connected system.

Primary strategic message:

“More patient demand requires more workforce capacity. Novalyte connects both.”
