20. ADD A CLINIC FAQ SECTION
==================================================

Add a comprehensive FAQ section near the bottom of the Clinics page, before the application-entry gate.

Recommended heading:

Frequently Asked Questions From Clinic Owners

Use an accessible accordion.

Include at least the following questions:

1. What is Novalyte AI?

Answer direction:

Novalyte AI is a healthcare technology ecosystem connecting men seeking care with clinics, healthcare professionals, educational resources, and operational services.

2. Is applying for the Clinic Directory free?

Answer direction:

Applying is free. Approval and listing are subject to verification. Optional patient-acquisition, enhanced visibility, workforce, marketplace, or software services may have separate fees.

3. Does applying guarantee that my clinic will be listed?

Answer direction:

No. Novalyte AI reviews clinic information, credentials, treatment offerings, and submitted content before publication.

4. What information is required to apply?

Answer direction:

Organization details, locations, decision-maker information, provider credentials, treatments, patient operations, directory content, and verification information.

5. Why must I verify my email or telephone number before accessing the full application?

Answer direction:

Verification helps protect the application process, reduce automated abuse, save the application securely, and confirm that Novalyte AI is communicating with a legitimate clinic representative.

6. Does Novalyte AI provide medical care?

Answer direction:

No. Novalyte AI is a technology platform. Clinics and licensed professionals remain responsible for diagnosis, prescribing, and treatment.

7. What is a treatment-ready patient opportunity?

Answer direction:

A patient opportunity may include treatment interest, goals, location, care preference, timeline, self-pay openness, validated contact information, and consent. It does not mean the patient is medically eligible.

8. Does Novalyte AI guarantee leads or revenue?

Answer direction:

No. Patient demand, lead volume, conversion, and revenue vary by treatment, market, clinic capacity, pricing, response time, and many other factors.

9. Can we choose which treatments we want patient opportunities for?

Answer direction:

Yes. Clinics may specify treatments, locations, states, telehealth coverage, patient capacity, and operational preferences.

10. Can telehealth clinics join?

Answer direction:

Yes, subject to verification, licensure, state coverage, and directory requirements.

11. Can multi-location clinics apply?

Answer direction:

Yes. The application supports multiple clinic locations, providers, services, and telehealth states.

12. What happens after the application is submitted?

Answer direction:

Novalyte AI reviews completeness, verifies information where appropriate, requests missing information if needed, prepares the clinic profile, and communicates approval or next steps.

13. How does clinic matching work?

Answer direction:

Potential matches may consider treatment interest, location, telehealth preference, clinic services, availability, and other operational factors. Matching does not determine medical suitability.

14. Can our clinic receive patient opportunities without purchasing an enhanced listing?

Answer direction:

Explain that directory listing and optional patient-acquisition programs are separate. Availability and commercial terms may differ by market and launch phase.

15. Can we update our profile after publication?

Answer direction:

Yes. Approved clinics should be able to request updates to providers, locations, treatments, hours, pricing information, and media.

16. What security controls protect our application?

Answer direction:

Explain secure transmission, verification, access controls, application tokens, rate limiting, secure uploads, and responsible data handling without making unsupported compliance claims.
