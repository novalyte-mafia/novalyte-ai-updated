# Investor Portal Content Gaps

Fields below are **intentionally unpublished**. Public and gated overview pages omit them rather than showing fake values. Status labels on the portal make remaining gaps honest.

## Founder inputs required

| Gap | Where it surfaces | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Founder biography (3–5 sentences) | Company page | Write + paste into `content.ts` / admin content |
| Founder photo | Company page | Upload approved headshot to `public/` or storage |
| Founder LinkedIn URL | Company / Contact | Add once confirmed |
| Headquarters / entity legal name | Company | Publish only after counsel confirms |
| Corporate structure (LLC/C-Corp, jurisdiction) | Company / Investment | Founder + attorney |
| Raise amount, instrument, cap/discount | Investment page | Currently `"Founder input required — not published"` — replace with deliberate “shared in conversation” copy or publish real terms |
| Use of funds detail | Investment / Financials | Enter via founder admin after deciding numbers |
| Financial scenarios / series | Financials | Enter only real or labeled projections |
| Traction metrics (Actual) | Traction | Seed via `/investor/admin/metrics` — clinics contacted, profiles live, assessments completed |
| Scheduling link (Cal.com / Calendly) | Meet / Contact | Set when ready |
| Approved product screenshots | Product | Capture sanitized screens from live directory, Campaign Studio, Command Center |
| Cited market sizing | Market | Add 2–3 entries with source URLs and `metricStatus` |

## Legal / compliance inputs

| Gap | Status |
|---|---|
| Counsel-approved forward-looking statements disclaimer | Placeholder text visible — replace before wide outreach |
| Published investor access terms version | Draft seeded; founder must publish via admin |
| Anti-kickback / lead-routing review before monetizing leads | Not a portal blocker; required before paid lead products |

## Data room (empty by design until upload)

Recommended first uploads:

1. One-page company overview PDF
2. Product architecture one-pager
3. GTM / clinic outreach plan
4. (Later) SAFE template after attorney review — never upload unsigned legal as “final”

## Rule

**Never invent** revenue, users, clinics, partnerships, investors, market share, fundraising commitments, or financial projections to fill these gaps.
